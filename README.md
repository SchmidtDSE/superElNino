# Super El Nino across scales

This project aim to analysis how the Super El Nino event of 1982/83 appears across climate data sets of various scales, resolutions, and origin in the Mojave Desert od the Desert Southwest

The datasets we are looking are:

### Measurements
- Rain gauge data acessed via NOAA

### Historical/ Reanalysis gridded products
- PRISM
- ERA5
- ERA5-Land
- Pierce et al. (https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/hydr/22/7/JHM-D-20-0212.1.xml)

### Historical runs from climate models
- CMIP6
- ISIMIP
- CalAdapt (LOCA2-Hybrid)

We are especially interested in how this event shows up at Joshua Tree National Park and Death Valley National Park, and if the extremely high rainfall in Historical gridded products is real or an artefact of their extrapolation from station data.
